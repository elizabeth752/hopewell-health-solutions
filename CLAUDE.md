## Settled facts (2026-11-18)

Resolved from the client's own sources without asking the client. Order of authority: the client's "Hopewell Website Updates" doc (office service lists, 2026-11-16), the Nov 2026 knowledge base, the live site (`origin/main`), the client wiki (`src/pages/review/wiki.astro`), `providers.js`, client files in `private/`, and the CTM call export (2026-05-16 to 2026-11-16; aggregate facts only, no caller details). Where sources disagree, the most recent client-authored source wins. Use these everywhere, including FAQ text (FAQ schema is built from it).

1. **TRICARE: accepted, but do not call it "in network".** Admissions staff tell callers "we do take TRICARE" (calls Jul to Nov 2026). Not every clinician takes it, and TRICARE Prime members need a referral from their primary care provider (staff, Nov 2026). The client wiki says "TriCare East = certified, not in-network", and the client doc asks for "We accept all major insurance providers" instead of TRICARE/PPO detail. So: "We accept TRICARE" on the rates page, with TRICARE left out of the "In network with" list. No "TRICARE East accepted as a PPO plan" lines anywhere.
2. **PHP hours: Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 3 PM** (six hours a day). Every admissions call that states the hours says "nine to three" (Aug to Nov 2026). The last timetable block is 1:30 to 3:00 PM. PHP is at 300 Hebron Ave only (client doc).
3. **Addiction IOP: Monday to Thursday, three hours a day, most people attend 8 to 12 weeks.** At 300 Hebron Ave: mornings 9 AM to 12 PM or evenings 5 to 8 PM. In Westbrook: evenings 5 to 8 PM for now (a day group is planned; ask admissions). It runs within the adult co-ed co-occurring IOP. Westbrook has no separate addiction-only group (calls Jul to Nov 2026; the 2025 Adult IOP flyer). This replaces the old "3 to 5 sessions a week, 6 to 8 weeks".
4. **Westbrook Women's Wellness IOP meets in the mornings, Monday to Thursday, 9 AM to 12 PM.** Sources: Kaitlyn Czapiga's bio (lead clinician for the Westbrook morning program, `providers.js`) and admissions calls (Oct 2026). An evening group in Westbrook is not confirmed, so say "ask about an evening option". 300 Hebron runs both mornings and evenings (client doc).
5. **The Women's Trauma IOP (Mon to Thu, 5 to 8 PM) and the First Responder IOP (Mon to Thu, 9 AM to 12 PM) stay listed, both at 300 Hebron Ave.** The live site has both, the client's 2025 service menu lists the First Responder IOP, and callers and referrers were being booked into the Women's Trauma IOP through Oct 2026. The client doc's "IOP" at 300 Hebron covers them. No call in the export mentions current First Responder enrollment, so add no new First Responder specifics.
6. **MAT is offered at 300 Hebron Ave and at Westbrook.** Sources: the client's Westbrook open house flyer ("Psychiatric Medication Management, including MAT"), the client doc (addiction care at 300 Hebron and Westbrook only), and the 2025 service menu. One prescriber gives Sublocade injections (call, Aug 2026).
7. **Play therapy: give no minimum age, and say "at select offices".** The sources conflict: the live site says ages 3 to 12, the KB says 5+, and admissions staff (Oct to Nov 2026) tell callers the youngest children they see are about 6. Clinicians trained in play therapy are on the Glastonbury and West Hartford rosters, and East Hampton has a play therapy room. Use "young children at select offices", never "every office" or "from age 4/3". The general "ages 4 and up" for counseling and medication management comes from the live site and stays (see open items).
8. **Licensure: no practice-wide "licensed in CT, MA, NY & FL" claim.** The client wiki says IOP is licensed in CT only, with medication management in CT, MA and FL. Staff say out-of-state telehealth depends on the clinician's license (calls, Aug and Oct 2026). Wording: "Our IOP and PHP programs are licensed in Connecticut. Some of our prescribers are also licensed in other states, so telehealth medication management may be possible…" (rates page text and FAQ).
9. **The "Outpatient Alcohol Rehab" and "Outpatient Drug Rehab" H1s stay.** The only wording rules in the KB, wiki and briefs are: "addiction IOP", not "substance use IOP"; "supportive accommodations", not "sober houses"; no outcome, "best" or guarantee claims; and addiction copy is allowed (wiki). Nothing bans "rehab", and "Outpatient" keeps the H1s accurate (the FAQs say there is no inpatient care). Keyword targets are unchanged.
10. **East Hampton offers 1:1 therapy and medication management only.** The client doc (2026-11-16) supersedes the KB's East Hampton teen/tween line. Staff tell callers the Teen and Tween IOPs are only at 1420 Main St, South Glastonbury, and that Westbrook teen groups are expected around January 2027 (not on the site yet).
11. **No MD/DO psychiatrist on staff.** The prescribers are PMHNP-BC APRNs and PAs (`providers.js`). Do not write "psychiatrist(s)" for Hopewell staff. Use "psychiatric provider", "psychiatric nurse practitioners and physician assistants", "psychiatric care" or "medication management". Location titles: Pratt St = "Glastonbury Mental Health Care at 33 Pratt St | Hopewell" (primary keyword now "glastonbury mental health"), West Hartford = "Therapy & Psychiatric Care in West Hartford, CT | Hopewell", East Hampton = "Therapist & Psychiatric Care in East Hampton, CT | Hopewell".
